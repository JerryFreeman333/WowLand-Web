Personal reverse engineering project recreating Google's TiltBrush VR painting application for WebGL and Augmented Reality environments. Meticulously analyzed and reconstructed all original TiltBrush brushes, particle systems, and visual effects through shader programming and instanced rendering techniques. Implemented instanced rendering systems for efficient particle management and post-processing pipelines replicating TiltBrush's signature aesthetic. Successfully translated VR-native painting mechanics to web-based AR interaction paradigms.
