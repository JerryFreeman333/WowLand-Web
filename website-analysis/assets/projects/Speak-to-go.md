## infos

Creative and prototyper for Google Creative Lab London developing voice-controlled WebVR travel application. Built innovative VR experience combining WebVR, Web Speech API, and Google Street View 360° imagery, enabling users to verbally navigate to any global location instantly. Developed seamless integration between speech recognition and immersive VR environments, allowing natural language commands like 'I'm feeling lucky' for spontaneous destination discovery.'
