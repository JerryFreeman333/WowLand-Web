## infos

WebGL Technical Director and lead GL developer for NAOZ, a year-round persistent digital entertainment venue built for Tomorrowland at Dogstudio. Architected a comprehensive WebGL festival engine featuring cinematic depth of field through painted blurred skyboxes, advanced glass shaders with dual reflection maps, and dynamic lighting systems. Developed custom browser-based editing tools for real-time camera positioning and lighting adjustments.
